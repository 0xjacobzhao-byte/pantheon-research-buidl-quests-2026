# Commercial Model

## 1. Executive Summary

Pantheon Research monetizes a governed, cross-asset research operating system.
The near-term model is built around **recurring software, research, and workflow
revenue** — subscriptions, packaged frameworks, research/data APIs, and
institutional licensing. Trading-related upside is deliberately treated as a
later-stage opportunity that becomes credible only after validated signals,
audited execution, and a real track record.

This document describes the revenue architecture, customer segments, packaging
logic, sequencing, moat, go-to-market motions, and risks. It contains **no
invented pricing, customers, revenue, AUM, conversion rates, or TAM figures** —
where a number is not verified, the *mechanism* is described instead.

## 2. Commercial Principles

- **Recurring first.** Software and research subscriptions before any
  performance-linked economics.
- **Sell governance, not tips.** The product is disciplined, explainable
  research — evidence packs, model comparison, data-quality states — not signals
  sold as guarantees.
- **Workflow lock-in over novelty.** Value compounds when Pantheon becomes part
  of a user's or team's research workflow.
- **Trading upside is earned, not assumed.** It is the highest-upside but
  latest-stage pillar and is gated behind validation.
- **Never overclaim.** No revenue, user, or performance figure is published
  until it is real and verifiable.

## 3. Revenue Architecture

Five pillars, ordered from nearest-term to latest-stage:

| # | Pillar | Near/Late | Primary mechanism |
|---|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Subscription Research Platform | Near | Recurring subscription |
| 2 | Investment Skills Marketplace | Near–Mid | Per-Skill / bundle / license |
| 3 | Research / Evaluation / Data APIs | Mid | Usage / per-report / enterprise |
| 4 | B2B / Institutional Licensing | Mid | Annual license + services |
| 5 | Long-Term Proprietary Strategy Upside | Late | Performance-linked (future) |

## 4. Subscription Platform

**Target buyers:** sophisticated individual investors, independent analysts,
crypto-native allocators, advisors, small family offices, small investment teams.

**Product:** the cross-asset dashboard (macro regime, equity research, BTC / ETH
/ DeFi, FICC, technical analysis, narrative research, Research Ops), AI research
overlays, model comparison, alerts, and research briefs.

**Revenue mechanism:** monthly and annual subscriptions, tiered product access, a
premium research tier, and team seats. Possible packaging — Explorer, Pro,
Institutional / Team. *Prices are not published until verified.*

**Current status:** product live; membership / payment foundations developed;
pricing and GTM validation in progress.

## 5. Skills Marketplace

Pantheon's deterministic frameworks can be packaged as **reusable, versioned
investment Skills** — Global Macro, US / China / Hong Kong / Singapore Equity,
Bitcoin, Ethereum, DeFi Yield, Technical Analysis, Narrative Trading, Fixed
Income, FX, Commodities.

**Target buyers:** advanced retail users, advisors, research teams, AI-agent
users, and developers integrating financial-research workflows.

**Revenue mechanism:** per-Skill subscription, Skill bundles, usage-based access,
enterprise licensing, white-label Skill deployment.

**Boundary:** a Skill is **not** a guarantee of returns and **not** an autonomous
trading strategy. It packages framework logic, data requirements, output schema,
risk gates, explainability, and update cadence.

**Current status:** framework base developed; packaging and marketplace
distribution on the roadmap; no marketplace revenue claimed.

## 6. Research / Evaluation / Data APIs

Three API product families:

- **A. Equity Evaluation API** — company quality, moat, cash engine, valuation,
  risk summary, evidence pack, LLM comparison, missing evidence, confidence,
  data-quality state.
- **B. Market Intelligence API** — macro regime, BTC / ETH state, DeFi verdicts,
  TA state, FICC views, signal history, readiness and freshness.
- **C. Data & Evidence API** — canonical observations, normalized snapshots,
  evidence artifacts, provider health, provenance, quality labels, and historical
  revisions where available.

**Target buyers:** fintech products, wealth-management apps, research tools, AI
applications, advisors, funds, internal investment teams.

**Revenue mechanism:** API usage, per-report pricing, monthly quota, enterprise
contracts, data licensing.

**Current status:** backend and research artifacts developed; public API
commercialization not fully launched; API packaging and SLAs remain roadmap work.

## 7. B2B / Institutional Licensing

Four product forms: **dashboard licensing** (team access, research modules, model
comparison, data-quality visibility); **custom research workflows**
(market-/asset-specific frameworks, custom evidence packs, institutional
reporting); **white-label research infrastructure** (embedded dashboard, API
integration, branded reports, client portal); and **premium services**
(implementation, provider integration, framework customization, analyst training,
research-operations support).

**Target buyers:** family offices, financial advisors, crypto funds, hedge-fund
research teams, asset managers, small institutions, fintech companies.

**Revenue mechanism:** annual licensing, implementation fee, professional
services, support retainer, usage fee.

**Current status:** product and infrastructure foundation developed; commercial
outreach and GTM in progress; no institutional customer is claimed.

## 8. Long-Term Strategy Upside

The latest-stage, highest-upside pillar. The progression is strict:

```text
Research Signal → Backtest → Forward Validation → Paper Trading
    → Attribution → Controlled Execution → Proprietary Strategy Upside
```

Possible future models: proprietary capital, a separately managed account, a
family-office mandate, strategy licensing, or performance-linked economics where
legally permitted.

> Trading profit is the highest-upside but latest-stage revenue pillar. It only
> becomes commercially credible after strategy-specific backtests, forward
> validation, paper-trading attribution, execution controls, and a real track
> record.

**Not claimed:** current trading revenue, current AUM, current audited
performance, or current live autonomous execution.

## 10. Packaging and Monetization Logic

- **Tiered access** separates casual (Explorer) from professional (Pro) and team
  (Institutional) usage.
- **Skills** convert framework IP into discrete, licensable units.
- **APIs** monetize the same governed artifacts programmatically without extra
  research cost.
- **Licensing / services** capture higher ACV where integration and support are
  required.
- Each layer reuses the same underlying research engine, so marginal cost of an
  additional surface is low.

## 11. Commercial Sequencing

- **Stage 1 — Software & research revenue.** Dashboard subscriptions, premium
  evidence digest, research briefs, model comparison, individual Skills, paid
  evaluations. *Goal:* validate willingness-to-pay, learn workflows, build
  recurring revenue, improve product-market fit. **← near-term focus.**
- **Stage 2 — Team & institutional workflow revenue.** Multi-seat plans, advisor
  and family-office workflows, APIs, white-label reports, custom research
  operations. *Goal:* higher ACV, deeper integration, lower churn, workflow
  lock-in.
- **Stage 3 — Validated strategy monetization.** Paper sleeves,
  broker-integrated approval, managed workflows, licensed strategies, proprietary
  capital. *Goal:* monetize validated signal quality and execution discipline.

## 12. Unit-Economic Drivers

Described as *mechanisms*, not figures:

- **Revenue drivers** — subscription seats, tier mix, Skill attach rate, API
  usage, license ACV, services attach.
- **Cost drivers** — LLM inference cost per completed analysis, data-provider
  cost, cloud cost, support cost.
- **Retention drivers** — workflow integration, alert relevance, data-quality
  trust, breadth of asset coverage.
- **Margin levers** — shared research engine across surfaces, caching of governed
  artifacts, provider cost control, offline/fallback discipline.

## 13. Commercial Moat

Stated as an **emerging / compounding** advantage, not a claimed defensible moat:

1. **Framework IP** — versioned methodologies and asset-specific domain knowledge.
2. **Governed Data** — provenance, freshness, revisions, provider health, evidence
   packs.
3. **Validation History** — backtests, forward outcomes, signal attribution, and
   failure records that accumulate over time.
4. **Multi-Model Research Corpus** — model agreement/disagreement, provider
   behavior, evidence gaps, and cost/quality history.
5. **Workflow Integration** — dashboards, APIs, alerts, Research Ops, and future
   broker workflow.

Unvalidated items are described as an *emerging moat* / *compounding asset* /
*intended advantage*, not a proven barrier.

## 14. GTM Motions

- **Product-led** — self-serve subscription, free/low tier to paid conversion.
- **Content / research-led** — public research briefs and model-comparison output
  as top-of-funnel.
- **Design-partner-led** — advisors and family offices co-developing workflows.
- **API / developer-led** — evaluation and data APIs embedded in third-party
  products.
- **Community / distribution** — Telegram and research alerts as retention and
  referral surfaces.

## 15. Risks and Dependencies

- **Willingness-to-pay** unproven → Stage 1 experiments before scaling.
- **Data rights / provider reliability** → provider routing, fallback, cost
  control.
- **Model cost / drift** → cost caps, schema validation, disagreement monitoring.
- **Regulatory scope** (especially any execution) → legal review gates before
  launch.
- **One-founder concentration** → AI-assisted operation, documentation, and
  automation reduce but do not remove key-person risk.

## 16. Near-Term Experiments

- Pricing and packaging tests on the subscription tiers.
- A premium research digest and paid equity-evaluation offering.
- Design-partner interviews with advisors and family offices.
- API pilots with a small number of integration partners.

## 17. Non-Claims

- No current revenue, users, AUM, conversion rates, or TAM figures are claimed.
- No institutional customer or design partner is claimed as signed.
- No pricing is published as final.
- No live autonomous trading and no performance track record are claimed.
- The public repository is a sanitized review slice; commercial infrastructure
  (payments, entitlements, billing) is not fully represented in public code.
