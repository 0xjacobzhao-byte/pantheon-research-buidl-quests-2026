**Project Name**
Pantheon Research

**Project Introduction**
Pantheon Research is an AI-native investment research operating system for public
markets. It combines structured market data, institutional-style investment
frameworks, deterministic signal engines, backtest analytics, data-quality
tooling, and multi-model LLM research overlays in one cross-asset platform. LLMs
interpret governed structured evidence (not raw prompts), multiple models are
compared to surface disagreement and missing evidence, and a human retains final
judgment. It is built and operated by a single founder.

**Core Team Background**
Solo founder — **Jacob Zhao** ([@0xjacobzhao](https://x.com/0xjacobzhao)) —
combining an investment background with AI and full-stack engineering. One person
owns research methodology, backend, frontend, cloud deployment, data operations,
and go-to-market, using AI-assisted development to reach institutional breadth.

**Project Innovation Related to AI**
Two separated layers: a deterministic framework layer (structured evidence,
scoring, signals, backtests, data-quality labels, content-hash audit trails) and
an LLM research-overlay layer (multiple models analyze the same evidence pack,
outputs are compared, disagreements and missing evidence are surfaced). Honest
`data_state` semantics and fail-closed provider handling mean the system never
fabricates a result — a missing key or bad output yields an explicit blocked
state, never a hollow success.

**Pain Point Solved**
Investment research is fragmented across disconnected tools, and single-model AI
research is unaccountable. Pantheon unifies cross-asset evidence, adds a
disciplined deterministic layer, compares multiple models, and keeps a human in
the loop — turning scattered data and raw LLM output into an auditable research
process a single operator can run.

**Current Development Progress**
Live web product (pantheon-research.com); multi-asset research modules; multi-
model LLM overlays with side-by-side comparison; Research-Ops / data-quality
tooling; a live cloud deployment (Alibaba ECS) with a secret-free proof endpoint;
ongoing backtest and forward-validation work. Roadmap items are not claimed as
complete.

**Expected Revenue Sources**
Subscription plans for individual investors; premium research tiers; paid
research / evaluation APIs; B2B / institutional licensing; reusable investment
"Skills". A controlled execution layer is a future possibility only after
validation. No revenue, user, or AUM figures are claimed today.
