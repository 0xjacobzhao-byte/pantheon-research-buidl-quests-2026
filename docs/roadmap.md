# Roadmap

Pantheon's roadmap follows one ordering principle:

> Strategy first. Information second. Signal third. Execution last.

Execution is intentionally the final stage, gated behind validation and risk
controls. Nothing below is claimed as live unless explicitly labeled.

---

## Now — Research OS and signal quality

*Status: active.*

- strengthen strategy backtests and forward validation;
- broaden data coverage and provider reliability;
- deepen five-model LLM evaluation and comparison quality;
- refine the signal alert layer and delivery;
- mature commercialization readiness (membership / payments).

## Next — Controlled research-to-execution workflow

*Status: roadmap.*

- a paper-trade journal with performance attribution;
- read-only broker integration;
- approval-based execution (human clicks, not autonomous);
- audit and risk gates around every action.

## Later — Constraint-bound execution

*Status: roadmap.*

- framework-specific position caps;
- risk overrides and a kill switch;
- reconciliation across accounts;
- multi-account support;
- jurisdiction-aware operations.

---

## What is **not** on the near-term path

- No live autonomous trading.
- No unattended execution.
- No performance, return, or AUM claims.

The trading layer is manual and human-controlled today. Any progression toward
constraint-bound execution depends on backtesting, forward validation, and risk
controls being in place first — not on a calendar.
